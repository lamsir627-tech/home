# Lighting And Color Optimization

Apply this reference after drafting each group in the resolved `15秒模式` or `30秒模式` and before final output. The three profiles below are creative references, not mandatory templates. Every group may use a content-matched custom lighting and color design when that better serves the storyboard emotion and environment.

Before this stage, [global-asset-control.md](global-asset-control.md) establishes the selected location geometry and plot-required time/environment variant. Fit the lighting to that version and its fixed windows/practical lights. A daytime image used only as a layout reference is not an available night image; record any required derived version or textual relighting design. Optimization cannot relocate windows, reset fire damage, change the scene's time or redesign an already matched asset to imitate a profile. Actual source/asset contradictions return to asset adaptation for a scoped resolution.

## Required Optimization Process

1. Read the group's environment, time, existing practical light sources, weather, materials, `情绪强度等级`, decisive microexpression shots, emotional turn, and neighboring-group continuity.
2. Assign exactly one primary class internally: `光线与色彩1`, `光线与色彩2`, `光线与色彩3`, or `自定义光线与色彩`.
3. Record a short internal `分类依据` using visible scene evidence. Do not classify from mood words alone when the setting contradicts them, and do not expose the class or basis in the standard final deliverable.
4. Design a physically coherent setup across the relevant dimensions: key light, fill/rim/environment light, palette, contrast/saturation, atmosphere, material response, and image texture.
5. Integrate the design into the visible `光线与色彩设计`, `场景与环境状态`, `视觉风格`, and key `镜头描述` blocks, then use the internal combined-prompt assembly only as a consistency check. Do not output a separate `合成提示词` field.
6. Output only the optimized group. Do not show the preliminary lighting-free draft.

Keep source-supported eyes, brows, mouth, jaw, and breath readable in decisive microexpression shots when the established light and framing permit. Do not change shot duration, shot size, camera position, blocking, or practical-source logic merely to display more facial detail; use the existing shot's readable features and let posture or breath carry the beat when the face is not legible.

## Classification Priority

Use this evidence order:

1. Explicit location, time of day, weather, and practical light sources in the script.
2. Existing environment materials and visible atmosphere.
3. The group's emotional function and emotional turn.
4. Continuity with adjacent groups in the same uninterrupted scene.

Keep one dominant class per group. If the group contains a justified lighting transition, describe the transition inside one coherent design and classify by the dominant final state. Do not switch profiles between adjacent groups without a visible reason.

Choose `自定义光线与色彩` whenever none of the three references fits cleanly. Name the custom direction briefly in the design, for example `阴天自然冷灰`, `正午硬光高反差`, `烛火暖暗低照度`, or `日落金蓝互补`. The custom design must still specify concrete, generatable light and color behavior.

## Reference Profile 1: 暖金高调柔光

Best suited to warm indoor portraiture, intimate dialogue, reassurance, gentle romance, celebration, domestic comfort, beauty-oriented close-ups, or other scenes that support soft warm practical light and low visual threat.

- 主光：右前上方的大面积柔光，面部阴影极弱。
- 辅光：正面柔和补光，提亮眼窝和皮肤。
- 轮廓光：发顶与右侧发丝有细腻高光。
- 背景：暖金色室内灯光，圆形焦外光斑。
- 色彩：奶油白肤色、蜜桃粉妆容、暖琥珀背景，辅以少量灰绿色。
- 质感：高调、低反差、低饱和，轻微柔焦与高光晕染。
- 对应关键词：大面积柔光箱，右前上方柔和主光，正面弱补光，发丝轮廓光，暖金色环境光，圆形散景，低对比度，奶油色调，蜜桃粉肤色，高光轻微Bloom。

Use only compatible details. Do not create warm indoor lamps, makeup colors, or circular bokeh when the established setting does not support them.

## Reference Profile 2: 冰蓝梦幻逆光

Best suited to cold night scenes, ethereal fantasy, ceremonial beauty, restrained romance, dreamlike revelation, elegant melancholy, or environments where blue-white backlight and delicate reflective fabrics are already plausible.

- 主光：冷白柔光照亮面部，柔和美人光。
- 轮廓光：强烈蓝白逆光，头发与肩部银白轮廓光。
- 环境光：深蓝夜景环境光，冷色调。
- 背景光束：斜穿画面的蓝白体积光束，丁达尔效应。
- 高光效果：柔光泛光，辉光溢出，梦幻Bloom。
- 材质表现：半透明薄纱，丝绸反光，刺绣花瓣细节。
- 色彩关系：冰蓝、银白、淡粉配色，低饱和冷调，高光微暖。

Use volume beams only when haze, mist, dust, or another visible medium is supported. Use gauze, silk, or embroidery response only when those materials exist in the scene or supplied reference assets.

## Reference Profile 3: 青蓝暗红工业低调

Best suited to industrial night scenes, crisis, pursuit, confrontation, danger, dystopian pressure, wet ruins, or other environments that support low-key contrast, cold backlight, contaminated surfaces, and limited red practical lights.

- 主色调：青蓝、黑色、少量暗红，teal-black-red palette。
- 光线方向：右上方强烈青蓝逆光。
- 人物照明：冷色侧逆光勾勒湿润面部，微弱面部补光。
- 明暗关系：低调照明，高反差，大面积暗部，深黑阴影。
- 空气效果：浓重烟雾，体积光，潮湿蒸汽。
- 环境材质：湿润金属，积水反光，油污，电缆与工业残骸。
- 红色点缀：远处红色警示灯反射，冷暖色冲突。
- 成像质感：变形宽银幕镜头，轻微电影颗粒，压暗黑位，真实胶片质感。

Use wetness, smoke, steam, metal, debris, or warning lights only when the established environment supports them. For a non-industrial threat scene, borrow only compatible qualities such as low-key contrast or restrained teal-red separation, or choose a custom class.

## Custom Design Rules

For `自定义光线与色彩`, design from the scene outward rather than from a preset. Cover at least these relevant dimensions:

- Key light: source, direction, size, hardness, intensity, and falloff.
- Support light: fill, negative fill, rim, bounce, environment light, or practical lights.
- Color: dominant palette, color-temperature relationship, saturation, and skin-tone handling when faces are visible.
- Tone: high-key/low-key, contrast range, black level, highlight behavior, and exposure priority.
- Texture: bloom, halation, diffusion, grain, lens character, or clean digital sharpness when appropriate.
- Atmosphere/materials: only effects and surface responses already justified by the script, assets, or established location.

Do not force all dimensions into every shot. State the group-level setup once, then add concise light behavior only to the shots where direction, transition, silhouette, reveal, reflection, or emotional emphasis matters.

## Final Audit

- Does the lighting match both the physical environment and emotional function?
- Is the selected class supported by visible evidence?
- If custom, is the design concrete enough to generate?
- Are light direction, color temperature, and practical sources physically coherent?
- Did the optimization avoid inventing weather, props, materials, smoke, mist, water, debris, or costume details?
- Do adjacent groups in one scene preserve continuity unless a visible transition occurs?
- Do the required visible fields contain the optimized design without classification notes or internal analysis, while the internal combined-prompt check remains consistent and unexposed?
