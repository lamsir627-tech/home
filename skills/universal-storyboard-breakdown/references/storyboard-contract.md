# 镜头时长、字段与质量契约

在详细分镜起草及最终核对时读取。本文件保留通用分镜的时长、字段与审美规则；入口与执行方式以 [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) 为准，用户交付格式以 [final-delivery-format.md](final-delivery-format.md) 为准。仅索引、资产整理或讨论技能时不加载本文件。

以下数值是本技能的制作规范，不是 Codex 或视频模型的能力上限。用户明确改写规范时记录新约定与受影响范围；不能因 Codex 升级自行放宽。所有用户参数（包括授权代选的画幅）以当前任务清单为准，缺失参数按入口解析。

## Duration-Conditional Grouping Rules

- Resolve exactly one group-duration mode for a new deliverable: default `15秒模式`, or `30秒模式` only when the current request explicitly asks for 30-second groups. A resumed script job reuses its verified mode unless the user changes it. Do not mix the modes inside one unlabeled deliverable.
- Use `第N组（目标MM:SS-MM:SS；实际X.X秒）` headings. Advance the target window by the resolved group duration and report the actual sum of the planned shots.
- In full-script mode, restart group numbering and target time at each source episode, use compound episode/group/shot IDs internally, and keep source episode boundaries immutable. Apply the reference's explicit final-short-group handling without borrowing dialogue from another episode.
- In every mode, apply the default workflow's final-incomplete-episode-group rule; it changes only the final group's real total, not per-shot caps or short-shot conditions. Batches within one episode continue existing numbers and target times.
- In `15秒模式`, each complete group should total `14.5-15.5秒` by default and each shot must stay at or below `3.5秒`.
- In `30秒模式`, each complete group should total `29.5-30.5秒` by default and each shot must stay at or below `5.0秒`.
- Treat `15.0秒` or `30.0秒` as the applicable target, not a mandatory exact sum, unless the user or platform explicitly requires an exact group duration.
- Inside `分段脚本`, use one block per shot: `【单镜头N】X.X秒`. Make the sum of all shot durations equal the reported actual group duration.
- Before calculating duration, read the dialogue's surrounding visual descriptions and apply [action/dialogue timing](source-text-and-shot-rules.md#3c-剧本动作与人物对话时序匹配): distinguish action before speech, action overlapping speech, and action after speech; segment mixed cases. Render the resulting order in visible action and sound cues, then calculate duration from locked source speech and continuous visible action. Use one-decimal precision when practical, but do not round below the natural audible delivery or truncate an action result.
- Durations do not need to be equal; assign each shot only the time required by its language and visible action.
- In `15秒模式`, if a long dialogue/VO/narration fragment needs more than `3.5秒`, split it at a natural semantic phrase boundary supported by a visible action milestone, emotional turn, spatial reveal, or camera-intention change. Prefer two consecutive shots when the full natural delivery fits within `7.0秒`; if two shots still cannot contain it, continue splitting at additional natural boundaries.
- In `30秒模式`, if a dialogue/VO/narration fragment's natural delivery exceeds `5.0秒`, split it at a natural semantic phrase boundary supported by a visible action milestone, emotional turn, spatial reveal, or camera-intention change. Prefer two consecutive shots when the full natural delivery fits within `10.0秒`; if two shots still cannot contain it, continue splitting at additional natural boundaries. Never accelerate, shorten, or rewrite locked text to force it under `5.0秒` or into a fixed shot count.
- Keep a continuous action in one shot when it fits within the resolved per-shot cap. If it exceeds the cap, cut only after a completed internal action milestone and make the next shot inherit the previous result, screen direction, body posture, eyeline, and prop state. If the action crosses a target group boundary, move the largest intact action unit and its attached source speech to the adjacent group when possible.
- Permit a shot under `2.5秒` only when it contains a naturally short locked spoken line. A short silent reaction, insert, establishing beat, or transition is invalid and must be merged into a neighboring shot.
- If a script beat is short because its locked spoken line is naturally short, it may remain a justified short shot. If it has no locked spoken line, combine it with the neighboring reaction, action, or transition.
- If a script beat is long, split it only at a natural phrase boundary paired with a speaker change, completed action milestone, emotional turn, spatial reveal, or camera-intention change. Preserve causal continuity across the resulting shots.
- Keep all source VO/旁白/台词 text intact and in order. A line may be split across shots, but each fragment must preserve the original words and punctuation.
- For paired bilingual lines, treat English as the single audible main track. Time, split, and lip-sync the English wording only; keep the paired Chinese translation in the internal ledger and never add its duration to the group total.
- Give every shot exactly one standard shot size: `大远景`, `远景`, `全景`, `中景`, `中近景`, `近景`, `特写`, or `大特写`. Put it in `镜头机位` for multi-character or zero-character shots and at the start of `画面简介` for single-visible-character shots.
- Audit the complete ordered shot stream with a rolling three-shot window. Three consecutive shots may not have the same focal subject. If they do, change one shot—prefer the third—to a feedback shot whose focal subject is another established character; the original speaker may continue on the locked main track as an off-screen voice. Re-run the window after every repair, including across group boundaries.
- Within a group, adjacent shots use different normalized sizes by default; actual character changes, clear viewpoint changes, genuine over-the-shoulder shot/reverse-shot, solitude, or source-supported emotional climaxes may exempt matching sizes under source-text-and-shot-rules.md section 5B. Small angle changes alone do not qualify; record actual exemption evidence. Across groups, end/start sizes must differ without these exemptions. Treat `中景双人` and `中景反打` as the same `中景`, and `过肩近景` as `近景`. Focal rotation and close-shot ratios remain separately checked. Nonadjacent reuse is allowed, as in `中景→中近景→中景`.
- Use no more than one long shot/wide shot/establishing shot per group unless the user explicitly requests slow cinematic pacing. Do not open with a slow wide shot. Build the first 2-second hook from the strongest action, prop, movement, POV, relationship tension, or justified facial reaction; do not default to a large-face close-up.
- In ordinary dialogue, deduction, and action groups, keep near shots, face close-ups, and large-face extreme close-ups together at no more than half the shot count. Use at most one large-face extreme close-up, defined here as a face filling roughly 60% or more of the frame.
- Do not place more than two near/close face shots consecutively in an ordinary group. Break the run with a medium shot, two-shot, relationship shot, over-the-shoulder, action framing, POV, or spatial insert.
- Character entrance, identity reveal, and necessary emotional buildup/turn/climax may justify more near or close shots. State the reason in `分镜自检`, vary scale and angle, and include at least one spatial or relationship anchor unless deliberate concealment is narratively required.
- Keep one stable lighting logic across groups that remain in the same uninterrupted scene. Change direction, color temperature, contrast, or atmosphere only when the script, location, time, practical lights, or emotional turn provides a visible reason.
- Keep one target aspect ratio across the deliverable unless the user explicitly requests multiple versions. When multiple ratios are requested, output a complete labeled version for each ratio or provide ratio-specific shot fields under every group; never mix two ratios inside one unlabeled combined prompt.
- Treat shot size as subject coverage, not as a crop instruction. Recompose camera position/distance, foreground layers, screen-space placement and tracking for the target frame while preserving normalized shot size, world-space blocking, movement paths and narrative content. Explicitly requested restaging uses a separate labeled and checked state chain.

## Output Format

Follow [final-delivery-format.md](final-delivery-format.md) exactly.

- Start with the plain title line `第N集｜本集核心冲突或反转标题｜画幅与方向｜分组时长｜视频质感风格`.
- In full-script mode, repeat the complete episode format for every requested episode; use one checked episode artifact per episode by default, with project records separate from the video prompts. Follow the full-script delivery extension in [final-delivery-format.md](final-delivery-format.md).
- Output the seven-field `全局连续性` block in its fixed order.
- Deliver the pre-storyboard asset tree/cards or supplied-asset binding table separately under [global-asset-control.md](global-asset-control.md); keep hashes, availability and audit records outside episode prompt fields.
- Final whole-script delivery also includes one separate, current character/location/prop prompt summary under global-asset-control.md section 6, containing complete prompt text and usage ranges for required asset versions. Reconcile it with the final storyboards and deliver it alongside them; preliminary asset packages or a link-only index are insufficient.
- Use `## 第N组（目标MM:SS-MM:SS；实际X.X秒）` headings.
- Output only the required visible group fields and the shot blocks.
- End every group with `负面提示词：`.
- Keep emotion/intensity matrices, lighting classification, combined-prompt assembly, and self-checks internal unless explicitly requested.
- In bilingual work, render paired dialogue as `英文主声轨（锁定）— ROLE: Exact English source line` and never render the paired Chinese translation as a second audible track.

## Shot Selection

Apply [分镜使用规则](source-text-and-shot-rules.md#5e-分镜使用规则) before filling the shot fields: one visible role is a single-character shot; two or more is a relationship shot. Prefer a speaker's single-character shot when compatible with focal rotation, preserve the character's actual blocking, and make speaking emotion concrete within the existing performance limits. Over-the-shoulder shot/reverse-shot follows these same design rules, while its visible foreground role still counts for the three-part format. A `多人站位关系镜头` needs at least three visible roles and a rotation-repair, large-action or blocking task; changing the visible headcount alone cannot repair a repeated focal subject. Keep type/task audit labels internal.

## Field Guidance

`生成任务` must summarize the complete reference plot of the current group in one short, natural, visible-beat sentence. Prefer two or three subject-action phrases and begin with `呈现`, `表现`, `刻画`, `展现`, `拍摄`, or `描绘`. Keep exact source boundaries in internal continuity records; do not quote the opening and closing lines, write `由……推进至……`, include ellipses or role labels, or use generic filler such as `生成一段写实中文短剧连续分镜`.

`主要主体` follows [the compact final-delivery format](final-delivery-format.md#主要主体人物场景道具简表): one line ordered as `人物名（简单服饰描述），人物名（简单服饰描述），场景名（状态），道具名（状态）`. Use actual names and current clothing/scene/prop states, with as many relevant entries as the group needs. Complete identity and appearance designs belong in the separate asset material; detailed blocking, injuries, contact and state changes belong in the scene/shot fields. This field stays compact whether or not references are bound, and does not replace the full independent asset summary or the group's spatial-entry requirements.

`场景与环境状态` must make the setting filmable: location, time, weather, lighting, crowd density, damage, furniture, vehicles, screens, doors, or other spatial anchors. Include the concrete incoming blocking under [full-script-mode.md section 3](full-script-mode.md#每组可独立使用的空间初态): fixed-anchor layout; each relevant character's anchor-relative position, facing, eyeline, posture/action phase and prop hand/contact; necessary distance/height relations and off-screen counterpart locations; established axis and physical camera side. Resolve nonadjacent returns from that scene instance's latest valid end state before writing this field. The first shot must start from it; camera/focal changes do not restage characters. For a multi-scene group, expand each scene's entry state at its own entry shot.

`情绪目标` must state how the complete current group guides the viewer's feeling. Use one concrete supported device such as sound, distance, concealment, approach, breath, gaze, prop reveal, action contrast, clue discovery, hesitation, impact, or release; name the emotional effect and, when present, its progression or landing. Keep exact start/end text in internal continuity records; do not quote it, write `由……落到……`, or use generic filler. Example: `用铁管摩擦声迅速建立威胁，再逐步揭示被追杀者与追击者的空间距离。`

`情绪强度等级` is an internal audit value containing exactly one label: `一级`, `二级`, or `三级`. Classify the whole group from a source-supported trigger, stakes, visible/audible leakage or control, emotional turn, and landing. `一级` covers restrained setup or low-risk undercurrent; `二级` covers a clear leak, pressure, control, or contained turn; `三级` covers a decisive source-supported shock, danger response, confession, breakdown, identity reveal, reversal, climax, or expression that functions as a plot event. Intensity never changes shot duration, shot count, group boundary, or close-shot allowance by itself. Do not expose this field in the standard final deliverable.

`情绪强度判定依据` is internal and must give one concise natural sentence using `触发事件 + 可见/可听反应 + 本组落点`. Do not quote boundary text, list adjectives, or justify the level through close-up count alone. Do not expose this field in the standard final deliverable.

`视频质感风格` must contain exactly one resolved label. For a new job, use `写实短剧质感` by default, or `高精度3D` when the current request selects it or a normalized synonym. A resumed script job reuses its verified style unless the user changes it. Keep the same choice across all groups unless the user explicitly requests separate labeled style variants. Apply the corresponding prompt rules in [video-texture-style-options.md](video-texture-style-options.md).

`光线质感分类` is an internal audit value containing exactly one primary class: `光线与色彩1`, `光线与色彩2`, `光线与色彩3`, or `自定义光线与色彩`. Use the supplied profiles when they fit. Choose `自定义光线与色彩` when a more specific design better serves the storyboard emotion and environment. Do not expose this field in the standard final deliverable.

`分类依据` is internal and must cite concise, visible evidence from the current group, such as time of day, location, practical light source, weather already present in the script, surface material, emotional turn, or continuity with the preceding group. Do not justify the class with generic labels alone or expose it in the standard final deliverable.

`光线与色彩设计` must describe a coherent, generatable lighting plan. Include the relevant key-light direction and quality, fill/rim/environment light, palette and color-temperature relationship, contrast/saturation, and image texture. Add atmosphere or material response only when the scene already supports it. Follow [lighting-and-color-optimization.md](lighting-and-color-optimization.md).

`视觉风格` must specify the resolved video-texture style, target aspect ratio, and camera language. For `写实短剧质感`, default to `写实短剧质感，目标画幅原生构图，自然表演，中景与关系镜头为主，近景和特写只用于人物出场、身份揭示或关键情绪节点，景别递进清楚，环境声真实`, replacing `目标画幅` with the resolved ratio and orientation. For `高精度3D`, identify the label and resolved ratio/orientation here, keep the shot grammar concise, then append the full fixed English positive block exactly once in this field as required by [video-texture-style-options.md](video-texture-style-options.md).

`分段脚本` must contain exact per-shot duration, locked source dialogue/VO/旁白 when present, concrete voice delivery, and clean visual instructions. Use the `【单镜头N】` block format. First count visible characters in the shot: exactly one visible character or animal uses only `画面简介`, with concise camera language integrated before its first semicolon; two or more visible characters or animals use `镜头与人物的位置关系`, `镜头机位`, `画面简介` in that order. Derive duration from the line's audible delivery and complete action unit, keep every shot at or below the resolved cap (`3.5秒` in `15秒模式`; `5.0秒` in `30秒模式`), and split long text at natural semantic and visible-action boundaries. Lock duration before microexpression design. Classify the local emotion event, apply the duration × event-level matrix, and integrate only the resulting source-supported changes into `画面简介`; the microexpression module cannot change timing. A sub-2.5-second shot must contain a locked short spoken line; never use a sub-2.5-second shot for `无` source text.

`镜头与人物的位置关系` is used only when two or more characters or animals are visible, or for a pure prop/detail shot using a画外 anchor. It must begin with `中心锚点角色：角色名。` Select exactly one visible person or animal who carries the shot's main action, speech, reaction, or narrative focus. State the anchor's screen position, facing direction, and eye-line endpoint first; then locate every other visible character relative to the anchor. For a pure prop/detail shot with no visible character, write `中心锚点角色：角色名（画外）。` and establish the prop along that role's facing axis. Omit this entire field for a single-visible-character shot.

`镜头机位` is used only when `镜头与人物的位置关系` is present. Define the camera relative to the same center anchor: front/side/back/shoulder-side position, height, pitch/side angle, shooting direction, exactly one standard shot size, movement, and focus subject. Omit this entire field for a single-visible-character shot; integrate its concise camera language into the opening of `画面简介` instead.

`画面简介` must state the concise visible content from action initiation through contact/change and result. Scene headings are routing metadata, not prose: do not reuse the full location label as the visible-content opening. Describe only the current shot's concrete micro-setting when it matters, such as the floor under a dragged prop, the bins used as cover, the door gap defining escape distance, or the light touching the active subject. In a single-visible-character shot, begin with `标准景别 + 基础拍摄角度 + 简单运镜或固定机位 + 焦点主体`, place a semicolon, then include the filmable background, character position and visible state, relevant props/light, complete action, and any selected duration-fitting microexpression transition. Example: `中景，平视侧前方固定拍摄，焦点落在角色上半身；昏暗门廊里……`. Do not add a separate position, camera, or microexpression field. In a multi-character shot, keep background, action, and focal-character microexpression here without restating camera geometry. Dialogue shots must use restrained, plot-functional performance after the dialogue audit.

`字幕/角色台词/旁白` is an audible source-text record field. Preserve the main-track dialogue, VO and narration exactly. Every audible line, including a single fragment, must use `SPEAKER: exact source fragment` with the reviewed ledger's speaker label; multiple fragments use separate lines in playback order. For paired bilingual source lines, output only `英文主声轨（锁定）— ROLE: Exact English source line`; keep the Chinese pairing internal as translation/subtitle reference, do not voice it, and do not time it again. Keep source `【字幕：...】` only in the internal ledger; do not put it into this rendered field, `镜头描述`, internal combined prompts, or generated on-screen text instructions. If a shot has no audible source text, write `无`.

The internal combined prompt must combine the core fields with the resolved video-texture style, final lighting-and-color, aspect-ratio design, and only the decisive source-supported microexpression transitions. Use it to consistency-check the visible fields, but do not output a separate `合成提示词` field in the standard final deliverable. When `高精度3D` is selected, include its exact positive block once and only once in the visible prompt/style content. Do not expose emotion-intensity analysis, microexpression complexity labels, surface/true-emotion notes, classification process, internal optimization stages, later cropping, or that the result was generated from a script.

`负面提示词` is always shown as the final line of each group. When `高精度3D` is selected, output the exact fixed negative block from [video-texture-style-options.md](video-texture-style-options.md); do not translate, shorten, reorder, deduplicate, or mix it into positive content. For `写实短剧质感`, leave the line empty unless the user or target platform explicitly requires a dedicated negative prompt.

## Quality Bar

- Make every group independently generatable while preserving series continuity.
- Complete the pre-storyboard asset route and retain traceable version usage: supplied references are inspected and selected, with actual binding status recorded; any claimed platform binding is verified, and unavailable binding uses the documented textual adaptation. Missing assets have usable prompt cards or explicit unresolved scope. Preserve identity/geometry while costumes, time-of-day, damage and possession follow source events. Keep asset text preparation, actual image availability and generated-video validation distinct.
- In every mode, require source-to-ledger integrity, reviewed classification, exact final rendered-dialogue coverage for the requested scope, separate action/continuity review, explicit incoming/outgoing scene-instance states, and a persisted resume position. Record actual file revisions and report scope. An episode-level pass is not a whole-script pass; unrequested episodes remain outside delivery coverage.
- Use English as the sole audible main track for paired bilingual scripts. Preserve English exactly for timing and lip sync, retain Chinese internally as translation/subtitle reference, and never voice or time both versions.
- Never allow the same focal subject to dominate three consecutive shots when another established character is available for a reaction. Repair the third shot first with that character's concrete feedback, keep the original speaker audible off screen when necessary, and repeat the rolling-window check across group boundaries. A scene containing objectively only one character is the sole exemption; do not invent a character to satisfy this rule.
- Match the exact final-delivery topology in [final-delivery-format.md](final-delivery-format.md), including the conditional reference-image field for second-round delivery: one plain episode title line, seven global-continuity fields, second-level group headings, required group fields and shot blocks, and a final `负面提示词：` line. Keep internal audit fields out of the standard final response.
- Carry emotional continuity across shots and groups: inherit the previous readable state, trigger, control attempt, intensity, and aftermath until a source-supported event changes them. Do not reset a character to a neutral face at every cut.
- Use the confirmed normalized aspect ratio consistently in `视觉风格`, ratio-specific shot geometry, and every combined prompt unless producing explicitly labeled multi-ratio versions. Do not add standalone `目标画幅` or `画幅与构图` output fields.
- Use one resolved `视频质感风格` consistently across the deliverable unless producing explicitly labeled multi-style variants. For `高精度3D`, preserve both fixed English blocks verbatim, include the positive block once per group, and keep the negative block in its own field.
- Recompose shots natively for the selected frame. Keep faces, hands, active props, contact points, entrances, exits, and visible action results inside a readable central action-safe area; use frame edges for supported environment, concealment, or movement lead space.
- Prefer concrete visible details over abstract commentary.
- Keep camera directions useful but not overloaded: one main camera intention per shot is enough.
- Keep multi-character three-part descriptions concise and non-overlapping. Keep single-character descriptions to one complete `画面简介` whose opening camera phrase contains one standard shot size, a basic angle, a fixed or simple movement instruction, and the focus subject, followed after a semicolon by background, character, and action.
- Keep exactly one center-anchor role in each multi-character or pure prop/detail shot. Do not create a center anchor, relationship field, or separate camera field in a single-visible-character shot.
- Keep dialogue-shot performance restrained: one necessary action or state change is usually enough; remove decorative micro-expressions and repeated gestures unless explicitly required by the source or a decisive emotional turn.
- Assign every group exactly one supported emotion-intensity level (`一级`, `二级`, or `三级`) and a concrete trigger/reaction/landing basis. Do not infer intensity from adjective count, close-up count, or prompt length.
- Lock shot duration, shot count, group boundaries, dialogue pace, and action milestones before microexpression design. The microexpression module cannot change any of them; only the storyboard-planning stage may reconsider timing when the expression itself is a source-defined plot event.
- Derive microexpression complexity from both locked duration and local emotion-event level, never duration alone. Use `final complexity = min(duration capacity, event demand)`: `≤2秒=极简`, `>2且<3秒=低`, `3-5秒=中`, `6秒以上=高` for duration capacity; `低/中/高` for event demand. Thus ordinary dialogue stays low even in a long shot, while a decisive betrayal reveal is high only when the locked duration can carry it. Allow simultaneous eye, brow, mouth, breath, or jaw changes without treating every descriptor as a separate time unit. The `6秒以上` capacity is external compatibility guidance and never overrides this skill's `3.5秒/5.0秒` mode caps.
- Prefer `starting state → concrete trigger → visible leak → control/landing` and distinguish surface emotion from supported true emotion through visible behavior. Keep one dominant progression; reject static labels and unordered large-emotion stacking.
- Apply full microexpression detail only when the face is readable. For wide, rear-view, occluded, prop-only, or zero-character shots, use readable posture/eyeline/breath or omit facial detail. In multi-character shots, prioritize the center/focal character and keep secondary reactions plot-functional.
- Keep every shot at or below the resolved cap: `3.5秒` in `15秒模式`, `5.0秒` in `30秒模式`. Let speech and action determine unequal durations, split long text at natural phrase boundaries, and preserve action-state continuity across the cut. In `30秒模式`, never accelerate or rewrite a line whose natural delivery exceeds `5.0秒`; split it at a natural semantic node.
- Do not let face close-ups or near shots replace blocking, spatial relationships, prop interaction, or action coverage. Use them as emphasis, not as the entire visual grammar.
- Each close-up must have a visible purpose such as character entrance, identity reveal, decisive reaction, emotional turn, or dialogue landing. Adjacent matching sizes also require a verified within-group exemption under section 5B; a generic purpose or slight angle change is insufficient.
- Fail matching adjacent normalized sizes within a group when no supported exemption applies, and always fail matching cross-group end/start sizes. Reframe the later shot first, then the earlier shot if needed; change actual coverage together with the label. Follow section 5B of source-text-and-shot-rules.md if replanning is necessary; boundary movement alone supplies no exemption evidence. Preserve locked text and all timing/action/spatial continuity, and recheck both neighbors, group boundaries, and overlapping focal-subject windows after changes.
- Do not use a dialogue split as the only difference between two shots. Adjacent fragments of one line must inherit the same physical state while advancing a distinct visual beat, camera intention, or audible cadence.
- Make `画面简介` read like a concise description of what the viewer sees. Do not paste or paraphrase raw scene headings at the opening, and do not use generic fillers such as `空间边界清楚` or `主要通行方向保持畅通`. Require concrete visible detail plus a visible state/action/change. One compact multi-clause sentence is valid; do not pad it merely to reach a sentence count. Do not use workflow phrases such as `作为可见背景`, `既定信息对象`, `动作结果`, `持物结果`, `落位`, or `支撑点`.
- Make `生成任务` read like a concise description of the group's whole visible plot. Reject direct start/end quotation, generic workflow wording, and descriptions reusable across unrelated groups.
- Make `情绪目标` read like concrete viewer-facing emotion design for the whole group. Require a grounded audiovisual or performance device and an emotional effect; reject direct start/end quotation, generic rhythm wording, plot-only restatement, and descriptions reusable across unrelated groups.
- Avoid generic phrases like 高级感、大片感, unless paired with specific lighting, lens, movement, or composition.
- Make the lighting serve both the environment and the emotional turn. Prefer a coherent custom design over forcing an ill-fitting reference profile.
- Do not paste an entire lighting reference mechanically into every shot. Carry the group-level logic through the key shots with concise, visible light behavior.
- Do not mix signature features from several profiles unless they form one physically coherent lighting setup. Preserve practical light-source logic and adjacent-group continuity.
- Avoid adding on-screen text, subtitles, watermarks, logos, UI, or readable Chinese text. Source subtitle markers are post-production references only and remain solely in the internal source ledger; they never enter rendered audio or shot fields.
- Do not describe BGM. Use only source dialogue/VO/旁白, concrete voice delivery, environmental sound, and visible actions.
- For abstract relationship words such as 对视、对峙、面对面, add a lightweight orientation anchor such as 过肩反打、侧面对峙、POV、画外、前景背影、左面向右/右面向左.
- If the source script includes sensitive, violent, or intimate material, keep the prompt within the user's requested narrative while avoiding gratuitous visual escalation.
